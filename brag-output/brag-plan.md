# Brag Plan: Fundicate
## Invocation
Default landscape 1920x1080, 22 seconds. Polished tone. Music and SFX on. Voice disabled (no --voice).
## Nine-question rubric
1. App: community networking, proposals, voting and transparent outcome records, with NEAR testnet wallet signup.
2. Strongest claim: Fund together. Decide together. (src/app/layout.tsx).
3. Visual hook: connected network nodes, violet/cyan on deep navy from network.css.
4. Actual UI: the real public wallet-only login screen, captured in a fresh unsigned browser context; no personal data.
5. Shortest satisfying story: 22 seconds to connect identity, participation and traceability.
6. Tone: polished; a confident community product film with generous readable holds and crisp camera moves.
7. Audio: vol-12 warm clean bed at 0.28, fade out; three low-risk interface/soft-impact cues. No narration.
8. Caption: Built Fundicate: connect with a NEAR testnet wallet, bring your community together, and make decisions with a visible trail.
9. Flow: connect wallet → propose and vote → inspect outcome history. Governance panels are explicitly illustrative demo reconstructions, not live transactions.
## Angle and hook
Your wallet opens the door. Your community decides what comes next. First frame: large 'Your people. Your voice.' with a source-inspired network in depth. No fake growth statistics, security guarantees, live payment claims or hosted URL.
## Visual identity
Background #0c111d; text #edf1ff; secondary #aab7d0; accent #b5a8ff; cyan #79dde8. Source network.css overrides globals.css. Source body font Inter falling back to Segoe UI; package local Segoe UI and Semibold as FundicateUI. Use oversized text and tangible UI surfaces, not generic SaaS slides.
## Storyboard
1. 0–3.27: 'Your people. Your voice.' connected source-inspired nodes assemble around the type. Small Fundicate mark. Hold text. Soft entrance.
2. 3.27–7.09: Actual login.png on a large product surface, cropped/reframed for readability. 'One wallet. You’re in.' The actual button 'Continue with NEAR testnet' stays visible. Tiny supporting 'Wallet-only signup'. A restrained pointer/click supports the action without claiming a successful live signature.
3. 7.09–12.02: Real feature structure recreated as an illustrative community proposal: 'Community garden', 'Proposal → Vote → Decision'. Two clear option surfaces, a vote highlight, and published decision state. Display 'Illustrative demo' clearly. No fabricated member ownership or claims of actual ballot results.
4. 12.02–16.93: 'Every decision. A visible trail.' A source-inspired outcome/activity record unfolds with Proposal / Decision / History; 'Illustrative demo'. No money transfers or financial balances.
5. 16.93–22: Fundicate logo/wordmark, source tagline 'Fund together. Decide together.' Closing 'Built for your collective.' Network resolves into the brand. Final settled hold at least 2.5 seconds. Poster candidate 19.5 seconds.
## Transitions and motion
Fast entrances then holds. Continuous network motif, matched violet surfaces, slight scale/reframe on product image. Never tween layout. Choose 2–4 atomic GSAP rules. No full-screen noisy gradients or stock footage.
## Music cue guidance
Read vol-12 preset. Beat grid 3.27,7.09,12.02,16.93; strong cue 13.11 and17.47 can land outcome and logo within +/-0.15s. Mark beat-locked and beat-grid code. Readability wins if timing conflicts.
## Audio treatment
Copy music and low-risk SFX to local assets. Use extracted PCM RMS to make existing network line/node presence subtly breathe, not an equalizer. SFX on the wallet button, proposal selection and logo. Fade music in/out. No voice.
## Preservation
All video output stays in brag-output. Do not modify application source, database, deployment or accounts. Login screenshot is public unsigned UI. All reconstructed community material is fictional and labeled.
