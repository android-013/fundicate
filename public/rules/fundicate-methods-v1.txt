# Fundicate methods V1 — frozen V4 election contract

Frozen before engine implementation, 6 November 2026. This specification applies only to new funded `rulesVersion:4` rounds. V1, V2 and V3 keep their published interpretation. All six methods, both selection weights and all three allocation policies are required; none is silently substituted.

## Configuration and ballots

`selectionMethod` is `FPTP`, `IRV`, `STV`, `ELO`, `GLICKO_2` or `FORCED_PAIRWISE`. `selectionSeats` is an integer 1–50 for STV, and exactly 1 for other methods. Seats are published before collection. If fewer candidates qualify, effective STV seats are the smaller of published seats and qualified candidates. No candidate means no award. Candidate IDs sort by ordinary code-point order, independently of database/input order. Maximum 50 candidates and 10,000 eligible voters.

Ballots are strict JSON objects. FPTP: `{schemeId:string}`. IRV/STV: `{ranking:string[]}` with 1–50 distinct qualified candidates, in preference order. Partial rankings are allowed; no unranked candidate is inferred. Pairwise methods: `{pairs:[{a:string,b:string,choice:"A"|"B"|"DRAW"}]}`. Each unordered qualified pair appears exactly once; `a < b` is canonical. Reversed, duplicate, foreign, same-candidate, missing or extra pairs are invalid. DRAW is allowed for Elo/Glicko-2 and forbidden for forced choice. For one candidate, exactly zero pairs is complete. Ballots with unexpected properties are invalid. Accepted ballots are immutable; a user submits exactly one complete selection ballot.

Every pairwise voter receives the complete, deterministic candidate-ID-ordered pair assignment: `n(n-1)/2`, at most 1,225 comparisons. The UI pages this workload and can hold a local in-progress draft, but the server counts only an atomically submitted complete ballot. No sampled or omitted comparisons count as complete. Participation/quorum counts each unique voter's frozen positive selection power **once**, regardless of comparisons. A submitted pairwise ballot never multiplies the electorate denominator.

Screening, eligibility, received-funds cutoff, optional veto and finance rules remain as in the V3 specification. EQUAL means each eligible voter has power 1. CONTRIBUTION means actual received eligible minor units at cutoff, with no cap or asset conversion. Zero-power and duplicate users are invalid. Quorum is ceiling(all eligible selection power × quorum basis points / 10,000). Empty participation cannot produce an award even if there is a single qualified candidate. Ballot rows and user identities stay sealed from other users until selection closes; results/transcripts are not computed for public reads before then. Own submitted ballot remains available to its author.

## IRV

Use whole BigInt voting power. Count each ballot's first continuing preference. Exhausted ballots retain their original participation for quorum but leave the active-majority denominator. A candidate with strictly more than half of active power wins. If all continuing candidates have equal totals, stop with a tied leading group; zero active power yields no supported winner. Otherwise eliminate one lowest candidate, transferring its full ballot power to the next continuing preference. If several share the minimum, eliminate the lexicographically greatest ID; publish the tied set and chosen elimination in the replay. This disclosed administrative tiebreak does not pretend the original totals differed.

The final continuing set ranks first, ordered by final tally with equal totals tied. Earlier eliminated candidates follow in reverse elimination order. An early majority winner ranks first; other still-continuing candidates follow by current tally; previously eliminated candidates follow in reverse order. No arbitrary ID breaks a monetary tie among candidates with equal totals in a final continuing group. Transcript includes each count, exhausted/active power, majority threshold, eliminated candidate and elimination tie set.

## STV

This is the explicitly named **Fundicate exact inclusive Gregory V1** variant, not a claim to reproduce every jurisdiction's STV rules. Quota is integer Droop: `floor(total submitted power / (effective seats + 1)) + 1`. The quota remains fixed as ballots exhaust. Rational values use reduced arbitrary-precision BigInt numerator/denominator, with no truncation, decimal rounding or lost transfer units.

At each count, assign each live parcel to its first continuing preference. Elect the candidate at/above quota with the largest exact tally, using smaller candidate ID for an exact election-order tie. Retain exactly quota and transfer **every** parcel in that candidate's pile at fraction `(tally - quota)/tally` of its current value. Remove the elected candidate from continuing. A zero surplus transfers zero. Nontransferable surplus becomes exhausted. When no candidate reaches quota, eliminate the smallest exact tally, using greatest candidate ID for an exact elimination tie, and transfer its entire current value. When continuing candidates equal remaining vacancies, elect them without requiring quota, retaining their current values; zero support is explicitly disclosed. Stop when seats fill. Track `retained + live + exhausted = initial submitted power` exactly after every event.

Elected candidates form the first monetary ranking tier, ahead of all nonelected candidates; within that tier equal exact tally **at election** ties, larger election tally ranks higher. Nonelected candidates follow: remaining continuing candidates by exact final tally, then eliminated candidates in reverse elimination order. Election/last-vacancy and elimination tie sets and ID decisions are visible in the transcript. The `elected` set is distinct from allocation: proportional/ranked money can include nonelected candidates according to the published support transform; a seat creates no automatic financial entitlement.

## Elo

**Fundicate batch Elo V1** uses one simultaneous round-level rating period, fresh rating 1500 for every candidate and K=32. For each comparison expected score is `1/(1 + 10^((opponent pre-period rating - own pre-period rating)/400))`; result score is 1, 0 or 0.5. Rating becomes `pre-rating + K × sum(frequency × (score - expected))`. Both candidates use pre-period ratings throughout, with no sequential feedback or input-order dependence. Equal weight is the ordinary batch sum with each comparison frequency 1. The named **paid-frequency extension** uses the voter's frozen paid minor-unit power as frequency, mathematically equivalent to repeated identical games, computed by aggregates rather than expansion. No rating is carried across rounds.

## Glicko-2

Use Mark Glickman's actual Glicko-2 period update, including rating, rating deviation, volatility, estimated variance, improvement, pre-period deviation growth and the stable Illinois volatility solver. Initial rating 1500, RD350, volatility0.06; tau0.5; epsilon0.000001. Maximum 1,000 iterations for both volatility bracketing and narrowing; failure rejects tally rather than returning an invented rating. Scale factor173.7178. No games leaves rating/volatility unchanged and increases RD using the published inactivity equation. Opponent pre-period values are used for all updates; all comparisons in a round constitute one simultaneous period. Standard equal-weight helper accepts arbitrary published pre-period opponent values and is tested against the official 1500/RD200 example: paper-rounded result1464.06, RD151.52, volatility approximately0.05999. Full-precision calculation gives rating1464.0506705393013, deviation151.51652412385727 and volatility0.059995984286488495; the paper rounds the intermediate mu to -0.2069 before its final conversion. Tests verify the unrounded oracle and reproduce that published rounded step.

The separately named **Glicko-2 paid-frequency likelihood extension V1** multiplies each comparison's information and score-residual terms by frozen paid minor-unit frequency. Equal frequency1 preserves the standard algorithm. Repeated equal opponent rows may be aggregated into one row with summed frequency and frequency-weighted result score; this is algebraically identical. Frequencies must be positive safe integers, with no silent clipping. The application's maximum received-funds bounds fit this domain; out-of-domain engine calls fail explicitly. This extension does not claim Glickman's endorsement. All candidate updates start from the same fresh baseline; public results include rating, RD, volatility, frequency and period metadata.

## Forced pairwise

Each pair has exactly one preferred candidate. Add the voter's power to the winner and subtract it from the loser. Report raw signed BigInt net scores and comparison coverage. The sum of all candidate raw scores is zero. There are no draws and no partial comparison votes. Higher signed score ranks first; equal signed scores stay tied. One-candidate complete ballots give a sole candidate an uncontested rank after positive participation.

## Ranking, support and all 36 financial cells

FPTP retains the V3 raw received/equal vote-power support and tied-count ranking. IRV, STV, Elo, Glicko-2 and forced pairwise use **RANK_POINTS_V1**: after the specified method produces its competition ranking, each candidate's nonnegative allocation support is `candidate count - rank + 1`. Equal method ranks receive equal support. If there are no submitted positive-power ballots, all support is zero. Ratings compare after rounding rating to 0.000001 rating points; RD/volatility are reported but never monetary support or extra tiebreaks. This avoids allocating money in proportion to raw ratings or negative pairwise scores. Rank points intentionally fund ranking positions rather than claiming to measure vote-share percentages.

Every method/weight combination supports the existing exact V3 allocator unchanged: WINNER_ALL gives a unique positive support leader the pool before the request cap, otherwise refunds; PROPORTIONAL apportions by these declared support units with largest remainders by scheme ID; RANKED_CUTS consumes prepublished 100% slots by rank, refunding all tied positions, missing positions, caps, invalid recipients and unused units without shifting/redistribution. Original-source refund ownership and veto gates are unchanged. This is an explicit separate money policy, including for STV. `workflow/method-matrix.json` names each of the 36 cells, its support transform and the test scenario IDs; references identify required executed evidence, not predeclared success.

Every result records config, unique participation/quorum, canonical candidates, support/ranks, elected IDs, and a deterministic JSON-safe replay transcript. BigInts/rationals serialize as exact strings. Canonical candidate/user/pair ordering makes permutation replay stable. Counts/transcripts do not include user identities; publication of individual ballots still follows the established selection-close boundary.

## Primary references checked before implementation

- [Glickman's Glicko-2 specification and official worked example, 22 March 2022](https://www.glicko.net/glicko/glicko2.pdf), used for the rating update and reference vector.
- [Australian Electoral Commission: Senate count](https://www.aec.gov.au/Voting/counting/senate_count.htm), used for quota, transferable preferences, surplus and exhaustion concepts; Fundicate's exact rational variant and tie rules are specified above.
- [Irish Electoral Commission: voting system](https://www.electoralcommission.ie/irelands-voting-system/), used for ranked optional preferences and transferable-vote concepts.
- [FIDE rating regulations, rating-period score residuals and K](https://handbook.fide.com/chapter/B022024), used as a primary rating-period reference. Fundicate's logistic batch Elo parameters are its declared polling specification, not a FIDE rating service.
